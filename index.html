How to play

Tap a group

Two or more touching squares of the same color smash on a single tap. Everything above tumbles down and fresh squares drop in.

Go big

Score is the group size squared. A ten-square blast is worth twenty-five times a two-square pop, so plan your pops.

Forge a Smasher

Smash six or more at once and a Smasher appears. Tap it to wipe out an entire row and column.

Beat the goal

Reach the score goal within twenty moves to clear the level. Every move you have left pays a bonus.

Why players love it

No ads, no timers

No energy meters either. Just the puzzle, playable offline anywhere.

Gallery-grade design

Flat Bauhaus colors, a framed canvas, and zero clutter. Juicy confetti, ink ripples, and haptics on every smash.

Share your score

Post a poster-style score card to Instagram, TikTok, X, Facebook, or anywhere else right from the level-complete screen.
