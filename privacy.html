Privacy Policy

Effective October 25, 2026

Super Square Smash ("the app") is developed by Remington Howden ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles. The short version: the app does not collect, store, or share any personal information, and it contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect personal information, usage data, device identifiers, location, or any other data about you. We have no servers that receive information from the app, and no accounts or sign-ins exist.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your best score in local app storage on your device so it can be shown the next time you play. This value never leaves your device except as part of a device backup you control. Deleting the app deletes it.

Photos access

When you choose to share a score card to TikTok or Facebook, the app asks for permission to add images to your Photos library so the card can be saved for you to post. This permission is add-only. The app cannot read, view, or modify any existing photos. You may decline the permission and still share through the system share sheet. The app never accesses Photos for any other purpose.

Sharing to other apps

Share buttons hand your score card and a caption to the app you choose using Apple's standard mechanisms: the system share sheet, the pasteboard, or a URL that opens the other app. Once another app receives the content, that app's own privacy policy applies. We do not receive any information about what you share or where.

Third-party services

The app does not include any third-party software development kits, analytics tools, advertising networks, or crash reporters. Apple may collect diagnostic and usage data under Apple's own privacy policy if you have opted in to sharing analytics with developers in your device settings; we receive only aggregated, anonymous information through App Store Connect in that case.

Children

The app is rated 4+ and is suitable for all ages. Because the app collects no information from anyone, it collects no information from children.

Your choices

You can revoke Photos access at any time in Settings. Deleting the app removes all data it stored on your device.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, this page will be updated and the effective date revised before the change ships.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the support page.
